# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

This repository brings together the project management processes used by OctoAcme so the team has a single place to understand how work is initiated, planned, executed, released, and improved.

## Project management process summary

OctoAcme follows a structured lifecycle for managing projects from idea to completion. Work begins with initiation, where the team validates the business problem, aligns stakeholders, and defines success metrics and a high-level timeline. Once the initiative is approved, planning turns it into an actionable backlog with scope, estimate, dependencies, milestones, and a clear Definition of Done. During execution, the team uses recurring team rhythms such as standups, weekly reviews, demos, and progress tracking in a project board to keep delivery moving and surface blockers early.

The project management model is built around clear roles and responsibilities. Product leaders define the outcome and backlog priorities, project managers coordinate schedules, risks, dependencies, and communication, developers build and test the solution, and QA validates quality and acceptance criteria. The team also relies on consistent stakeholder communication through weekly updates, milestone reviews, and structured escalation paths when issues cross team or business boundaries. This creates accountability and visibility across product, engineering, and leadership stakeholders.

Quality is treated as a continuous practice rather than a final gate. OctoAcme expects PRs to include issue links and acceptance criteria, automated validation through CI, security scanning, and at least one approval before merge. Depending on the work, teams apply unit, integration, and end-to-end testing, with manual QA for critical user flows. Release readiness is confirmed through smoke tests, verification, and documented rollback/incident planning before deployment. After each milestone or incident, teams conduct retrospectives to capture what went well, what can improve, and which action items should be converted into follow-up work.
